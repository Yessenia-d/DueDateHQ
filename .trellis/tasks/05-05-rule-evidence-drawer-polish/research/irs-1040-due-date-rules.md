# IRS 1040 Due Date Rules

## Question

Are the Rule evidence drawer's calculated Form 1040 due dates accurate for the currently displayed tax years 2026 and 2027?

## Sources

- IRS, "When to file": https://www.irs.gov/filing/individuals/when-to-file
- IRS Topic no. 301, "When, how and where to file": https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc301
- IRS Publication 509 (2026), "Tax Calendars": https://www.irs.gov/publications/p509
- D.C. Law Library, D.C. Code § 1-612.02: https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/1-612.02.html
- DC DMV 2026 holiday schedule, District Personnel Manual observation note: https://dmv.dc.gov/page/2026-holiday-closing-schedule

## Findings

- IRS says calendar-year federal individual income tax returns are generally due April 15.
- IRS says if the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day.
- IRS Publication 509 defines "legal holiday" for this adjustment as a legal holiday in the District of Columbia and states that its calendars adjust for Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays.
- IRS says Form 4868 gives an automatic six-month extension of time to file and must be filed by the original due date. The extension is not an extension of time to pay.
- D.C. Code lists District of Columbia Emancipation Day as April 16 each year.
- D.C. Code and the DC DMV holiday schedule note that when a legal public holiday falls on Sunday, the Monday immediately following is the observed legal public holiday; when it falls on Saturday, the Friday immediately before is observed.

## Application To Current Drawer Examples

- Tax year 2026 Form 1040:
  - Base due date is April 15, 2027. This is a Thursday, so no weekend or DC holiday adjustment applies.
  - Extension date is October 15, 2027. This is a Friday, so no adjustment applies.
- Tax year 2027 Form 1040:
  - Base due date is April 15, 2028. This is a Saturday.
  - April 16, 2028 is DC Emancipation Day and falls on a Sunday, so the observed legal public holiday is Monday, April 17, 2028.
  - The due date should therefore move to Tuesday, April 18, 2028.
  - Extension date is October 15, 2028. This is a Sunday, so it moves to Monday, October 16, 2028.

## Conclusion

The screenshot's 2026 row is accurate. The 2027 row's extension is accurate, but the 2027 row's due date is not: it should be `Apr 18, 2028`, not `Apr 17, 2028`.
