# DueDateHQ Context

DueDateHQ is a tax deadline operating system for solo and independent CPAs serving mixed individual and small-business clients across one or more states.

Product promise:

```txt
Know what is due, why it is due, and whether the source is verified.
```

Core product rule:

```txt
Only `Verified Tax Rule` records can create official system-generated `Deadline Task` records.
User-provided deadlines can appear in the user's workspace, but must be marked as not verified by DueDateHQ.
Needs review, Source changed, Unsupported, and Coverage gap items must be transparent but cannot be treated as confirmed deadlines.
Official Notice Monitor analysis can propose changes, but must not mutate CPA workspace data without CPA confirmation.
```

## Glossary

### CPA

A solo or independent tax professional using DueDateHQ to manage deadline work for mixed individual and small-business clients.

### Firm

The CPA's workspace. Business data such as clients, imports, deadline tasks, and user-provided deadlines belongs to a firm.

### Client

Informal shorthand for a person, business, household, or related group served by a CPA. Use `Client Relationship` when the relationship record matters.

### Client Relationship

The CPA's relationship with a person, business, household, or related group. It owns relationship-level identity and notes, and one client relationship can have one or more filing/tax profiles.

### Filing Profile / Tax Profile

The specific individual or business tax context used to match obligations and rules. Filing/tax profiles hold tax-relevant data such as entity type, state exposure, county, fiscal year type, notes, and import source.

### CSV Source Adapter

A source-specific importer for client data from systems such as TaxDome, Drake, Karbon, and QuickBooks. It normalizes source rows into the shared DueDateHQ client shape.

### Import Batch

A single CSV import attempt. It tracks source system, preview and commit status, row counts, header detection, rows needing review, duplicates, and adapter version.

### Tax Obligation

A known filing, payment, extension, or compliance obligation for a jurisdiction, agency, tax category, and entity type. A known obligation is not automatically a verified deadline.

### Tax Rule

A deadline rule attached to a tax obligation. It stores the due date rule, verification status, official source evidence, version, and verification timestamps.

### Verified Tax Rule

A tax rule whose official source and rule interpretation have been reviewed and are safe to use for official system-generated deadline tasks.

### Needs Review Rule

A candidate tax rule that exists but has not completed verification. It cannot generate official deadline tasks.

### Source Changed Rule

A previously verified tax rule whose official source changed and needs re-verification. It cannot generate new official deadline tasks until reviewed.

### Unsupported Obligation

A known obligation that DueDateHQ does not schedule yet. It should be visible in coverage views but cannot be treated as a confirmed deadline.

### Coverage Gap

A visible unsupported or unverified source, state, category, or obligation combination where DueDateHQ does not yet provide verified schedulable coverage. Coverage gaps must stay transparent without implying complete verified 50-state coverage.

### Deadline Task

The work item a CPA sees and triages. Official system-generated `Deadline Task` records can only come from `Verified Tax Rule` records; user-provided deadline tasks may appear in the workspace but must be marked as not verified by DueDateHQ.

### Official Deadline Task

A deadline task generated by DueDateHQ from a verified tax rule. It carries source evidence, verification status, and rule version.

### User-Provided Deadline

A manually entered deadline supplied by the CPA. It can appear on the dashboard but must be clearly marked as not verified by DueDateHQ.

### Firm Target Date

An optional internal planning date chosen by the CPA or firm. It helps prioritize work but is never an official due date and must stay visually and semantically separate from current and original official due dates.

### Deadline Date Event

An audit/event record for date changes on a deadline task, such as official extensions, official relief changes, user-provided adjustments, and firm target date changes.

### Official Source

A government or agency source used as evidence for tax rules. It may be HTML, PDF, RSS, API, or manually tracked.

### Source Snapshot

A captured representation of an official source at a point in time, usually keyed by content hash.

### Source Check Run

A monitoring run against an official source. It records check status, HTTP status, current and previous content hashes, change detection, and errors.

### Rule Change Candidate

A detected source change or extraction result that may affect a tax rule. Human verification is required before publishing an updated verified rule.

### Official Notice

A notice from an official source that may affect deadlines, coverage, or review status. Official notices can be analyzed and matched to local profiles, but analysis alone cannot change a CPA workspace.

### Notice Impact Proposal

A proposed task update or coverage/review status update created from official notice analysis. It requires CPA approval, rejection, or a decide-later decision before any workspace data changes.

### Verification Queue

The workflow surface where source changes, needs-review rules, and rule change candidates are reviewed before publication.

### Evidence Drawer

The product surface that shows why an official deadline exists, including source URL, source name, verification status, last checked time, last verified time, and rule version.

### Coverage Matrix

A product surface showing obligation and rule coverage across jurisdictions, agencies, tax categories, entity types, verification statuses, monitor status, and coverage gaps. It must make supported coverage explicit without promising complete verified 50-state coverage.

### Monday Triage Dashboard

The default working surface for CPAs. It prioritizes `Overdue`, `Due this week`, `This month`, and `Long range` deadline sections, with filters by client, state, entity type, tax type, task status, and verification status.

### Task Status

The operational work-progress status of a deadline task. Beta statuses are `Not started`, `In progress`, `Waiting on client`, and `Done`. Extension handling is a separate date concept, not a work-progress status.

### Waiting on client

A task status for work blocked on client-provided information, documents, decisions, or payment. It is distinct from `In progress` and from extension/date handling.

### Extended (date state)

A derived date state indicating the task's due date has been changed via an official extension. Extended is not a task status; it is determined from `Deadline Date Event` records with `official_extension` type. A task can be extended and simultaneously have any work-progress status such as `Waiting on client` or `In progress`.

### Overdue

A derived date state indicating the task's current due date has passed and the task is not done. Overdue tasks appear in a dedicated dashboard section above `Due this week`.

### Due Date Rule

A structured rule attached to a tax rule that defines how to calculate due dates. Beta supports fixed-date rules, quarterly rules, and extension date rules. The rule includes weekend/holiday adjustment. A pure function `calculateDueDate(rule, taxYear, fiscalYearEnd?)` interprets the rule and returns concrete dates.

### Task Generation Window

The time range for which deadline tasks are generated from verified tax rules. The window covers the current tax year plus the next tax year. Tasks with due dates before today are generated and marked overdue so the CPA can triage them. Tasks for prior tax years are not generated.

### Feature Progress Page

A product and review surface that shows which Beta capabilities are complete, partial, or pending.

## Avoid These Ambiguities

- Do not use "deadline" alone when the distinction matters. Prefer `Official Deadline Task`, `User-Provided Deadline`, or `Tax Rule`.
- Do not imply that a `Tax Obligation` can create tasks. Only a `Verified Tax Rule` can create official tasks.
- Do not call user-provided deadlines "verified" unless DueDateHQ has completed verification.
- Do not treat source monitoring as automatic tax rule publication. Monitoring creates candidates; humans publish verified rules.
- Do not treat official notice analysis as automatic workspace mutation. Notice impact proposals require CPA confirmation before changes are applied.
- Do not imply complete verified 50-state coverage in Beta. Show supported coverage, unsupported items, needs-review states, and coverage gaps explicitly.
- Do not treat `Extended` as a task work-progress status. Extension is a date state derived from date events; work-progress statuses are `Not started`, `In progress`, `Waiting on client`, and `Done`.
